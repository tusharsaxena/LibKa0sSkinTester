## The title-bar pass (rulings 3 and 4)

One window this addon draws itself has a title bar: the **skin showroom**
(`modules/Showroom.lua`, `LibKa0sSkinTesterShowroom`). Its bar carries one control, the close X,
built by `Core.MakeCloseButton` with this addon as the owner (`core/CoreSetup.lua`). The re-cut
library gives that close everything the rulings ask, so the pass needed no source change and no
`W.HeaderButtons` adoption:

- tooltip: "Close" (`Tooltip.CloseTip`), hooked by `AttachTitleTip`, placed by `PlaceAttached`
  (above, flipping below), in the owner's `gtip.title` (Core minor 18, `SR-L2`);
- colors: `close.rest`, which now follows `icon.tint` in every skin, at rest, and `close.hot` under
  the pointer (`SR-L3`). The showroom's own `R.Paint("CloseButton", close)` hooks the same pair and
  keeps the tooltip hook.

Two cases in `tests/test_showroom.lua` pin it: every title-bar control (the bar's and its band's
clickable children, the close included) shows a non-empty tooltip in `gtip.title` under every skin,
and the X rests in `icon.tint`, lights `close.hot` and returns to `icon.tint` under Ka0s Default and
every other skin. Both were checked falsifiable: with `Core.lua`'s `AttachTitleTip` call disabled the
first goes red, and with the first cut's `SkinDefs.lua` and `SkinDefsOthers.lua` the second goes red
(Azeroth's X rests in its old `close.rest`). The payload was restored byte-identical after each check.

Not a window: the Addon surfaces section's "Settings-style window" is a specimen inside a showroom
cell. Its stock close has its click removed and only shows how `S.CloseButton` paints the template's
button, so it gets no "Close" tooltip that would promise an action it does not take. Its colors are the
library's `S.CloseButton` pair, so they follow `icon.tint` and `close.hot` like the real one.
